The short version

You should be able to read a website without being followed around the internet. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, loads nothing from third parties, and has no accounts or forms. We don't know who you are, and we don't try to find out.

Server logs

Our hosting provider, Google Firebase Hosting, keeps standard technical logs to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Those logs record basic details like your IP address, the time, and the page you requested. We never use them to identify or profile you.

If you email us

If you write to us, we keep your message and email address only so we can reply and follow up. We won't add you to a mailing list, and we won't share what you wrote without your permission. If you ever want us to delete your emails, just ask and we will.

Interactive labs

Our interactive workbenches in Labs, including Shared Horizon and the Observatory, run directly in your browser with the same zero-cookie, zero-tracker standard. To see why local data custody anchors our architecture, read our vision or browse common questions. See our cookie notice, terms of use, or contact page if you have questions.

Changes

If our practices ever change, we'll update this page and tell you clearly so you can decide what you're comfortable with. Last updated: November 6, 2026.
