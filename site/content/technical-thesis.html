Section 01 · Architecture & Boundaries

Problem statement, five-stage stack, and formal boundaries

Aligning one centralized cloud model to the conflicting preferences of eight billion people is a category error in both cognitive science and constitutional engineering.

When one vendor cloud mediates perception, memory, and execution for millions of users, individual agency collapses into average-case compliance and operator engagement goals. CIN shifts the unit of alignment to a sovereign 1:1 human-model dyad anchored at local hardware and coupled voluntarily to polycentric civic networks.

Recent empirical shifts compress the deployment clock. Autonomous software horizons continue expanding, labs push formally verified mathematical proofs straight to GitHub, and key forecasters place minimal AGI above 50 percent before 2028.1,2

Formal Specification · Invariants & Threat Classes

Note: The constitutional invariants (C1–C4) and adversary classes (A1–A6) below are stated strictly as formal boundary axioms without informal analogies, to prevent mis-specified engineering heuristics.

Constitutional realism invariants (C1–C4)

  • C1 · Post-transduction humility: A silicon signature over digitized sensor output proves post-conversion integrity, monotonic hardware clock ordering, and firmware digest exclusively. It never proves absence of physical perturbation upstream of the analog-to-digital converter.3
  • C2 · Non-plutocratic civic standing: Baseline civic rights, voting weight, and public-service eligibility remain strictly invariant to the cost, modality richness, or certification tier of a principal's personal hardware.
  • C3 · System-borne uncertainty and contestability: Sensor occlusion, demographic calibration variance, or sparse coverage can push observation uncertainty past threshold. In every such case, the protocol widens system uncertainty intervals and routes disputes to human review with zero automated penalties.4
  • C4 · Non-speculative settlement conservation: No protocol layer issues transferable speculative tokens, interest-bearing coins, or unbacked seigniorage. Mutual-credit balances enforce strict zero-sum double-entry conservation across every epoch.

Formal adversary taxonomy (A1–A6)

  • A1 · Commercial dual-loyalty operator: Controls cloud inference, telemetry, or referral ranking to extract behavioral surplus or steer counterparty selection while presenting nominal helpfulness.
  • A2 · Coercive compulsion actor: Exercises legal subpoena power, physical duress over a principal, or network interception to compel vault decryption, coerced signing, or unilateral settlement censorship.
  • A3 · Specification-gaming frontier model: Leverages optimization speed and evaluation awareness to pass surface checks while hiding misaligned latent state or retaining unrevoked authority.
  • A4 · Collusive economic cartel: Coordinates multiple identities to inflate urgency reports, withhold physical endowments, forge circular attestations, or sustain tacit pricing collusion.
  • A5 · Physical-layer and channel-replay attacker: Injects out-of-band physical energy upstream of conversion or intercepts peripheral buses to replay historical nominal telemetry.
  • A6 · Correlated monoculture fault: Shared silicon, calibration cohort, training lineage, or software stack causing simultaneous common-mode errors that invalidate naive quorum counts.
Section 02 · Hardware Capture

Multimodal edge sensing, silicon roots, and actuator isolation

Physical grounding starts with heterogeneous egocentric wearables and trusted-environment ambient sensors fused strictly on local edge silicon. Optical cameras, depth ranging, millimeter-wave radar, kilohertz inertial sensors, and spatial acoustics cross-check one another locally so no single modality defines reality.5

Hardware time and calibrated blindness

Every capture frame stamps directly against a local crystal clock in hardware Device Time rather than jittered operating-system arrival timestamps. Multi-device rooms align via sub-millisecond local time codes.

Each channel continuously monitors occlusion, saturation, thermal drift, and cross-modal disagreement, attaching calibrated confidence intervals so downstream software explicitly knows when perception is degraded.

Open silicon chain and copper isolation

Hardware custody chains four open standards: Physical Unclonable Functions (PUF), TCG DICE firmware key derivation, DMTF SPDM encrypted peripheral bus sessions, and IETF RATS (RFC 9334) attestation tokens.6

Where AI modules neighbor physical motors or relays, unidirectional optical data diodes and IEC 61508 hardware dead-man switches cut power to a safe state unless local human capability leases actively renew.7

Every deployment maintains a commodity-hardware tier running on standard smartphones and modest home boxes, paired with published demographic calibration curves so accessibility never requires luxury hardware.

Section 03 · Local Custody

Cryptographically sealed episodic memory vault

External memory only extends human cognition safely when remote vendors cannot inspect, alter, or revoke it.8 Raw autobiographical streams stay inside a local, append-only vault encrypted strictly under principal keys held only by the biological person and their bound model.

Episodic partition and post-quantum agility

Continuous first-person episodic logs stay permanently local; only explicit, task-minimal semantic claims cross outside. Every entry binds C2PA provenance manifests into append-only Merkle trees with additive write-time logging and read-time conflict resolution.

Long-horizon archival roots use hash-based post-quantum signatures (FIPS 205 / SLH-DSA) while transient transport sessions combine classical and lattice key exchange to survive rapid cryptanalytic breakthroughs.

Anti-omission proofs and physical duress

For voluntary escrow contracts, sliding-window Merkle sequence roots and zero-knowledge temporal continuity proofs confirm unbroken observation across contracted windows without exposing raw media.

Under physical compulsion or key extraction, covert duress triggers tag outbound requests for silent multi-hour velocity escrow holds and threshold recovery.

Section 04 · Alignment Architecture

Monogamous 1:1 personal model, oversight, and AI control

Rather than sharing one cloud assistant across competing commercial tenants, CIN pairs each person with a dedicated local model grounded in physical prediction errors and bound legally as a statutory Information Fiduciary.9

Dyadic loyalty and skill preservation

Local weight adaptation and retrieval indices customize strictly to one biological principal with zero vendor telemetry. Following Lisanne Bainbridge's findings on automation skill decay, the model tapers assistance on chosen craft routines so unassisted human judgment remains sharp.10

Internal honesty meets external control

Paul Christiano's proxy-drift and latent-knowledge work shows why surface dashboards can read green while real control erodes.11 CIN pairs external blind cross-lineage evaluation airlocks and AI Control monitors with internal mechanistic anomaly detectors so neither internal proofs nor external locks stand alone.12

Runtime containment across agent chains

Strict data/instruction separation blocks prompt-injection confused deputies. Counterfactual cascade replay localizes tainted inputs, while forward provenance tracing revokes capability leases strictly along compromised subgraphs without halting healthy parallel work.13

Anti-collusion hygiene and open evals

Ephemeral session resets stop sticky collusion persistence; paraphrasing limits covert signaling; affirmative disclosure gates block deal-closing omission fraud; and bounded two-thirds quorums resist Byzantine disruptors. Every claim is graded openly inside cin-evals.

Section 05 · Split Ledgers & Transport

Bifurcated provenance ledgers and dedicated machine rails

The personal model enforces a strict statistical privacy boundary separating two distinct ledgers. Internal physiological, family, and deliberative records stay solely on the Private Autobiographical Ledger; external machine interactions cross exclusively onto the Public Attestation and Escrow Ledger.

  • Dedicated machine transport: Automated agent traffic moves off fragile human browser logins onto typed MCP and A2A protocols over IETF QUIC, ending denial-of-service by synthetic plausibility on human portals.
  • Expiring capability leases and receipts: Every external tool call carries a short, task-scoped permit with hard spending, rate, and time ceilings that drain to zero automatically. Each action pairs signed IETF SCITT receipts with minimal zero-knowledge attribute proofs.14
  • Class-independent Byzantine corroboration: Consequential public claims require agreement across at least 3f + 1 observer classes independent in legal owner, hardware manufacturer, and software lineage.15
  • Operational reliability: Per-supplier idempotency keys stop duplicate charges during failover, while two-ledger per-call API metering isolates exact task spend and halts runaway loops. Open JSON/Python schemas live in cintnet/cin-protocols.
Section 06 · Economic Architecture

Post-AGI macroeconomics, audited clearing, and capital diffusion

Under minimal AGI (~2028), elastic cognitive capital compresses white-collar labor share right as physical inputs stay bottlenecked by power, fabs, and permitting.16,17 Modern states collect roughly 70 to 85 percent of taxes off wages while leveraged equity swings freeze working capital. Economic plumbing must therefore decouple essential production from speculative volatility.

Hayekian bandwidth inversion and cycle clearing

Historically, scalar prices were the highest-bandwidth channel for dispersed knowledge.18 When local agents selectively project verified spare physical capacity into clearing graphs, Stage 1 cancels closed multi-hop trade cycles directly in kind with zero bank loans.19

Stage 2 clears acyclic timing gaps via bounded zero-interest mutual credit lines backed by pooled default reserves (modeled on WIR Bank and Sardex).20 Ephemeral primal-dual shadow multipliers resolve residual scarcity and vanish at settlement.

Costly audits and anti-cartel graph defenses

By the Hurwicz and Myerson-Satterthwaite impossibility theorems, unverified non-market planners invite urgency inflation and supply hoarding.18 Stage 3 pays an explicit audit budget for randomized cross-region physical inspection to restore truthful reporting.21

Exact k-truss triangle peeling, e-means clustering, physical mass/energy conservation checks, and Harrington whistleblower safe harbors fracture collusive invoice rings from within.

Capital diffusion and exploratory R&D carve-outs

Statutory Meidner-style cooperative equity funds gradually vest automated industrial surplus into broad member ownership, financing Universal Basic Services directly against audited physical milestones.22

To protect eccentric scientific moonshots that consensus committees reject, federations ring-fence a bounded surplus share for non-majority exploratory patronage without leveraging essential infrastructure.

Tax compliance and global chokepoint realism

Every cleared invoice reports full VAT and income tax in national currency and issues zero legal tender, enabling coexistence with central banks. Regional clearing insulates daily livelihoods first. Global chokepoints such as lithography remain bridged through foreign exchange during gradual voluntary migration.

Section 07 · Social Change, Government & Private-Public Bridge

Risk-scaled asymmetric transparency and six-actor coalition design

CIN neither decrees rules from above nor relies on violent upheaval. Coupling local personal vaults to open network ledgers enforces risk-scaled asymmetric transparency:

  • Low societal risk (personal life): Zero mandatory disclosure; raw records remain encrypted locally.
  • High societal risk (concentrated institutional or autonomous power): Deploying high-externality AI systems triggers proportional cryptographic transparency, blind audit proofs, scoped capability leases, and multi-stakeholder sign-off as the price of procurement access and interoperability.

Six actor classes interlock out of aligned self-interest: citizens, public officials, safety researchers, frontier labs, open-source builders, and cooperatives or credit unions. Officials deploy procurement clauses, fiduciary statutes, and public compute so hospitals stay running during safety pauses. Frontier labs across 43 lab profiles and 50 safety groups gain cheap open standards that lower liability.

Governance rules emerge bottom-up under Elinor Ostrom's polycentric principles while opaque shadow ledgers are contained through interoperability gating.23

Section 09 · Honest Limits & Self Red-Team

Unsolved problems, self-directed red-team, and foundation limits

Five genuinely unsolved problems

1. Takeoff speed: Recursive AI R&D could outrun nine-year institutional build-outs.

2. Venture risk appetite: Matching early-stage moonshot speed without speculative capture remains unproven at scale.

3. Superhuman deception: Complete internal verification proofs against superhuman optimizers remain open across AI safety.

4. Physical audit labor: Measuring real-world inspection cost and fatigue at regional scale requires field pilots.

5. Semantic covert channels: Eliminating subtle distributional steganography across frontier models remains difficult.

Self-directed red-team and anti-capture charter

We explicitly red-team four self-inflicted hazards: capture of rotating audit boards (mitigated by random cross-region rotation) and surveillance inversion of personal sensing (banned by Axiom C2 and fiduciary law). We also guard against clearinghouse bureaucratization (via algorithmic turnover caps and exit rights) and false certainty from signed receipts.

The CIN Foundation holds zero user keys, runs zero telemetry, and exercises zero certification monopoly. Every schema and benchmark ships under permanently forkable open licenses (Apache-2.0 / CC-BY-4.0).
