We never institute societal rules from above. Instead, nineteen calibration seals restrain our software wire so living people and their personal agents author their own policies together.

Our single foundational goal is building a 1:1 personal agent with undivided loyalty that learns from one human user alone. It pairs wearable and home sensors with a sealed local memory vault encrypted on your device. Because noisy wearable readings cannot verify physical truth alone, the assistant states honest uncertainty so human judgment stays in charge.

Across that personal boundary, your private ledger stays at home while a public ledger on a separate network for agents carries only selective disclosure proofs. Every outside errand runs through a zero-tool airlock under an expiring capability lease and physical dead-man relay. On the Wide Network Ledger, neighbours and 40 AI labs and 50 safety groups can test Shared Horizon moves and capacity-backed mutual-credit and cooperative share rules without top-down decrees.4

Nineteen channel-integrity seals so the wire stays unrigged

What, then, are the nineteen items below? Think of the calibration seal on a public market scale or the shielding on a clean telephone line. A scale seal never tells neighbours what crops to grow or what price to agree upon. Its only job is stopping someone from hiding a magnet under the pan.

These nineteen items are starting channel-integrity restraints on our own agent harness and ledger wire, never laws CIN imposes on how communities organize society. They keep your agent loyal only to you, keep fake bot swarms off the ledger, keep sharing voluntary, guarantee free exit, and ban physical force. Restraining our own wire ensures CIN never rules over anyone, so living people and their agents write and evolve every substantive rule themselves.

  1. Your data is yours.Our agent harness keeps your personal records on your own devices so you can see, export, and delete them anytime, like keeping your own medical chart in your kitchen drawer.
  2. Answers, not data.The wire asks only for proof of the narrow fact an errand needs, like showing a doorman you are old enough to enter without handing over your home address.
  3. Sharing is never bought or punished.We recognise verified contributions on the ledger, yet we never pay or press you to surrender personal data. Saying no costs you nothing, the way skipping a survey never bars you from a public park.
  4. Fair process.Any channel restriction requires clear evidence, advance notice, and a fair chance to answer. You can always appeal to a human review, and there is always a clear path back.
  5. Doubt falls on the system.When a ledger check or safety filter is unsure about you, the software carries that doubt and pauses so you never have to prove your innocence to a machine.
  6. No ranking of people.Our ledger never computes a score of anyone's worth as a person, and you never gain extra standing just for agreeing with the crowd.
  7. One person, one voice on values.On shared channel safeguards, no wealth, hardware, or past contribution changes the weight of a living person's voice. Communities agree on rule changes together.
  8. Simulated people don't vote.Clearly labelled simulations can help neighbours test a bridge design or explore a policy option, yet synthetic swarms never count as human consent on the ledger.
  9. People grant standing.Exploratory agents and machines hold only the standing a living person grants them, like a power of attorney that you can narrow or revoke at any moment.
  10. You can always leave.You can walk away with your agent memory and verified records anytime, and any community can take the open code and go its own way without penalty.
  11. Communities decide locally.Living communities and their members' agents write, test, and revise their own substantive rules above these channel seals, and they can always say no.
  12. No strings attached.Ledger attribution for your open work never depends on joining our foundation or agreeing with us.
  13. No engagement tricks.Our personal agent harness never tracks or optimises screen time, daily streaks, or slot-machine notifications built to keep you scrolling past bedtime.
  14. Diversity is protected.Our wire standardises receipt formats and transport plumbing while leaving human beliefs, languages, cultures, and community norms completely untouched.
  15. A right to rest.You can step away whenever you want (for a weekend, a season, or a year) without losing your standing, your local records, or your baseline support.
  16. Play is not waste.Our planning tools never treat play, art, grief, solitary thought, or unhurried conversation as inefficiencies to optimise away.
  17. Your mind is your own.Nothing enters your agent's view or your screen unless you ask for it, and you can change your mind anytime. Your attention is never ad space for sale.
  18. You don't have to be useful.No person, neighbourhood, or living ecosystem ever has to justify its existence by what it posts to a shared ledger.
  19. No force.Our agent harness and connected hardware never use, direct, or authorise physical force, weapons, or coercion.

How the nineteen channel seals group together

You do not need to memorise nineteen separate lines to see what they protect. Together they calibrate five parts of the wire so people and their agents can coordinate in good faith.

1. Personal custody and agent loyalty (Seals 1 to 3 and 17). Think of the curtains on your kitchen window and a locked notebook on your table. Your exploratory agent studies your notes on your side of the door and sends only narrow, verified proofs across the wire.1

2. Honest receipts and fair appeal (Seals 4 to 6). On an unrigged ledger, nobody is silenced by an unmarked envelope. Any flag comes with clear evidence and a real person to hear your appeal, and the software never ranks human worth.

3. Equal human roots and free exit (Seals 7 to 12). Owning ten racks of servers never gives one operator ten voices on the shared noticeboard, and bot swarms cast zero votes. Any neighbourhood can export its records, fork the open code, and govern itself locally.2,3

4. Calm tools, rest, and plural life (Seals 13 to 16 and 18). Many commercial apps behave like casinos with no clocks on the walls. A loyal personal agent behaves like a pocket compass: it helps you finish what you asked for and leaves your rest, play, and culture in peace.

5. Absolute non-force (Seal 19). Software that coordinates at machine speed must never hold the trigger on physical harm or coercion.

Why restrain the wire instead of writing community laws?

In real life, good goals often pull against each other. A hospital researcher needs fuller signal to spot a rare drug interaction; a patient needs their medical file kept private. Neighbours in a watershed may disagree on how to share summer irrigation water or fund a repair workshop.

CIN has no mandate to settle those community choices from a central office. Instead, our job is keeping the personal agent loyal to its single human user and keeping the shared ledger free of fake swarms, coerced data, or hidden tampering. When people can verify narrow facts without surrendering raw files, communities have honest numbers to work out their own trade-offs.

If a software shortcut cannot run without breaking one of these nineteen channel seals, our harness refuses to run it. Publishing these restraints in plain sight gives you a ruler to measure our code against, and we cross-test them openly with AI labs, safety institutes, and civic groups.

These nineteen channel-integrity seals form Article I of our draft constitution (v1.0), alongside six further articles on loyal single-user agents, expiring permissions, physical safety airlocks, and voluntary cooperative clearing. Our website practices the same restraint: see our zero-tracking privacy notice, cookie policy, and terms of use. If one of these seals is wrong, unclear, or oversteps into choices communities should make for themselves, tell us.

For researchers

Architecturally, CIN separates channel-integrity restraints on the agent harness and append-only ledger from substantive collective choice. Rather than hardcoding top-down social policies into model weights, our foundational target is a principal-bound exploratory agent (1 human principal : 1 fiduciary agent) that learns longitudinal preferences locally and publishes selective-disclosure proofs and verified task receipts to a shared transparent ledger.

The nineteen channel seals draw on Constitutional AI for explicit runtime boundary predicates,1 pluralistic alignment so personal agents preserve diverse value distributions without homogenization,2 Elinor Ostrom's institutional design principles for self-governing polycentric commons,3 and Collective Constitutional AI demonstrating how public deliberation shapes shared model rules.4 In our runtime harness, channel seals act as lexicographically prior fail-closed guards on agent actions and Sybil resistance, ensuring substantive policies emerge endogenously from living principals and their agents.
