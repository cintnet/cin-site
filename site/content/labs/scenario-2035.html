Most public discussion about artificial intelligence flips between vague utopian slogans and fictional anecdotes. A macroeconomist and a generalist prediction system work differently.

They start with accounting identities, physical constraints, institutional incentives, and base rates. They then trace conditional probabilities across mutually exclusive futures and name observable signposts that update those odds.

Baseline Premise · Minimal AGI by ~2028

In economic terms, minimal AGI does not require omniscient superintelligence. It means a general-purpose cognitive substitute that completes multi-week, non-routine remote professional workflows at or above median human competence. Marginal inference costs fall far below OECD wages, while physical supply chains remain bounded by real hardware and energy lead times.1,2

Five structural forces governing 2026 to 2035

Before trajectories branch, five macroeconomic and institutional pressures act on every major economy regardless of local politics.

01 · Factor Shares & Aggregate Demand

The wage-compression and tax-base paradox

In task-based growth models, elastic cognitive capital substitutes rapidly for white-collar labor while raising returns to scarce complements: compute clusters, power grids, fabs, land, and proprietary distribution.3

Modern economies rely on wages for roughly 55 to 65 percent of household consumption and 70 to 85 percent of sovereign tax revenue. Productive capacity rises right as household solvency and tax bases contract.

02 · Physical Bottlenecks

Bifurcated growth under Baumol constraints

A generalist forecaster rejects overnight tenfold GDP claims. Even if software and cognitive analysis approach zero marginal cost in 2028, physical output stays rate-limited by transformers, chip fabs, permitting, and robotics deployment.4

From 2028 to 2031, economies bifurcate sharply. Deflationary abundance in digital services runs alongside inflationary scarcity in electricity, housing, skilled physical trades, and clinical execution.

03 · Financial Plumbing

Valuation whiplash and bandwidth inversion

Financial markets price 10 to 30 year cash flows, yet autonomous cognitive competition can obsolete entire service sectors inside 18 months. Leveraged compute debt amplifies equity sell-offs into working-capital freezes across solvent real-economy firms.5

Simultaneously, privacy-preserving local agents and multilateral clearing engines invert Hayek's 1945 constraint.6 A single speculative ticker becomes a lossy, noisy channel compared to direct verified capacity clearing and bounded mutual credit.7,8

04 · Institutional Interfaces

Collapse of human-speed spam filters

Banking, courts, procurement, insurance claims, peer review, and public regulatory dockets implicitly rely on human cognitive effort as a rate-limiter. Dropping the cost of plausible filings near zero creates denial-of-service by plausibility.

Institutions face a forced architectural fork: lock access inside closed corporate identity monopolies, or separate machine traffic onto cryptographic channels with expiring capability leases and signed receipts.9

05 · State Capacity & Control

The sovereign fiscal and pause trap

Falling wage taxes and rising transfer demands collide with sovereign bond yields pushed up by trillions in private AI capital expenditure. Central banks face an impossible squeeze between inflating physical bottlenecks and crashing leveraged credit.

If proprietary AI clouds become embedded in tax collection, treasury clearing, logistics, and defense first, states lose leverage. They cannot pause or audit a frontier lineage without triggering an economic crash.10

The 2028 to 2035 Futures Cone (priors summing to 100%)

A generalist prediction system refuses single-track determinism. Below are three mutually exclusive structural trajectories for how economies and states adapt after minimal AGI arrives. Inspect all three side by side or isolate one path.

Trajectory A · 40% Prior

Leveraged oligopoly and managed dependency

Legacy financial markets and three to five vertically integrated compute consortia absorb minimal AGI without architectural reform.

Capital & Financial Plumbing

Autonomous trading dominates public exchanges, turning asset markets into hyper-volatile pools controlled by mega-cap balance sheets. Distressed mid-market firms are rolled up during recurring credit squeezes.

Labor & Sovereign Fiscal Base

Labor's share of GDP drops steeply. Governments patch collapsing wage-tax bases with monetized debt and narrow subsistence transfers while capital ownership concentrates further.

Institutional Interfaces

To survive synthetic spam, banks and ministries standardize on closed corporate identity ecosystems and standing vendor credentials. Switching providers becomes practically prohibitive.

Governance & Pause Capacity (2033–2035)

Headline metrics stay bright green even as real democratic agency erodes. Because proprietary AI clouds run settlement and public administration, states cannot order a safety pause without crashing daily life.

Trajectory B · 35% Prior

Bottleneck stagflation and institutional gridlock

Physical constraints, leveraged capex busts, liability paralysis, and geopolitical fragmentation stall broad economic conversion.

Capital & Financial Plumbing

Overbuilt data-center debt triggers painful credit deleveraging cycles before enterprise cash flows catch up. Capital retreats into defensive sovereign debt and hard commodities.

Labor & Sovereign Fiscal Base

White-collar displacement hits unevenly while physical shortages in power, housing, and care keep inflation elevated. Central banks oscillate between fighting inflation and bailing out commercial credit.

Institutional Interfaces

Courts, regulators, and healthcare systems react to automated fraud with blunt moratoria and manual bottlenecks. Adoption bifurcates sharply between lightly regulated niches and frozen public sectors.

Governance & Pause Capacity (2033–2035)

Fragmented national export controls slow diffusion without solving verification. Public trust in both markets and state agencies decays under persistent stagflation.

Trajectory C · 25% Prior · CIN Corridor

Open clearing and decoupled institutional transition

Working-capital freezes and portal overload push real-economy sectors and public buyers to build open clearing, diffused ownership, and verifiable machine rails.

Capital & Financial Plumbing

Regional firms and public utilities settle physical trade cycles through audited multilateral clearing and bounded zero-interest mutual credit.7,8 Speculative markets shrink back to bounded high-risk venture niches.

Labor & Sovereign Fiscal Base

Long-horizon infrastructure is financed via verified multi-year demand and Meidner-style cooperative capital funds that diffuse automated equity broadly.11 Productivity gains fund shorter hours and civic services instead of austerity.

Institutional Interfaces

Machine traffic moves off human logins onto signed channels using expiring capability leases, tamper-evident receipts, and blind cross-lineage evaluation airlocks.9,12

Governance & Pause Capacity (2033–2035)

Because essential livelihoods and working liquidity no longer depend on leveraged equity tickers or private AI clouds, regulators can enforce physical audits or pauses without starving Main Street.

Mechanism note: Stopping urgency gaming inside non-market clearing

Any non-price allocation faces Hurwicz incentive gaming, where participants exaggerate urgency to jump queues.6 Pairing hardware-attested physical inventory logs with randomized costly verification audits restores truthful reporting at modest overhead.13

Bayesian Early-Warning System

Four observable 12 to 24 month signposts

A forecast is only scientific if reality can prove it wrong. Watch these four measurable indicators between late 2026 and late 2028 to update trajectory odds.

Signpost 01 · Labor-Share Divergence

Output rises while white-collar hiring drops over 25%

Suppose OECD hiring in software, legal, and financial roles drops over 25 percent year-over-year by late 2027 while output holds or climbs. Acute fiscal pressure then arrives before 2030, shrinking Trajectory B while intensifying A and C.

Signpost 02 · Working-Capital Decoupling

Multilateral B2B trade netting scales during a credit freeze

If regional trade-credit netting or cooperative mutual-credit rings clear meaningful real-economy volume during a commercial credit tightening by end of 2028, Trajectory C shifts upward from 25 percent toward 40 percent.

Signpost 03 · Machine-Traffic Standards

Public procurement mandates scoped leases over vendor SSO

If major ministries mandate open expiring capability leases and blind third-party audit receipts by 2028, Trajectory C strengthens materially. If agencies standardize on closed vendor SSO clouds, Trajectory A rises above 55 percent.

Signpost 04 · Capital-Ownership Response

Debt-funded stipends versus diffused equity funds

When sovereign wage-tax shortfalls hit in 2028 and 2029, defaulting to debt-financed subsistence stipends locks in Trajectory A. Enacting broad equity-vesting funds and public compute capacity shifts odds strongly toward Trajectory C.

Stress-Testing Trajectory C

Five hardest economic and governance objections

Trajectory C currently carries a 25 percent prior rather than a majority because five structural challenges remain genuinely difficult.

1. Can non-speculative capital fund radical, non-consensus R&D?

The objection: Mutual credit and cooperative funds excel at legible physical needs like logistics, energy storage, and healthcare. Speculative capital markets, despite bubbles, let eccentric founders pitch fifty skeptics until one funds an unproven breakthrough.

Current status: Partially addressed. Ring-fencing a dedicated share of cooperative surplus for pluralistic, non-majority exploratory grants avoids leveraging essential infrastructure, yet matching venture risk appetite without reintroducing speculative capture remains open.

2. Will central banks and tax ministries tolerate parallel clearing?

The objection: Once multilateral clearing rings reach regional scale, finance ministries may view them as tax-leakage channels or threats to monetary transmission during inflation.

Current status: Manageable only via strict statutory integration. Switzerland's WIR Bank and Sardinia's Sardex endured because every cleared transaction reports full VAT and income tax in national currency and issues zero fiat legal tender.8

3. How does regional clearing handle ultra-concentrated global chokepoints?

The objection: Regional rings can net domestic food, freight, maintenance, and clinical care. They cannot clear extreme-ultraviolet lithography tools, rare-earth refining, or transoceanic shipping locally.

Current status: Genuine structural boundary. Open clearing acts first as a shock-absorbing domestic floor protecting daily livelihoods from financial panics, while federated international trade corridors mature much more slowly.

4. What prevents audit overhead, collusion, or local capture?

The objection: Physical verification audits consume real labor. If local cliques capture rotating audit panels, or verification lags behind machine-speed netting, cheaters drain default reserves before detection.

Current status: Active research frontier. Hardware-attested meter custody, turnover-based credit caps, randomized cross-region auditor rotation, and fast local circuit breakers shrink exposure, yet field trials must bound real-world overhead.

5. What if recursive AI R&D outruns a nine-year institutional build-out?

The objection: If automated research compresses decades of breakthroughs into two or three years after 2028, municipal clearing federations and open standards bodies may still be half-built when the hardest shock hits.

Current status: The sharpest timing risk in the forecast. That is why deployable near-term fuses (expiring capability leases, blind evaluation airlocks, and network separation for agent traffic) must ship immediately before deeper capital reforms finish.
