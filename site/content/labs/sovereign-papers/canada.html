Section 01 · Current Regulatory Architecture & Capture Risks

Legislative delays widened that accountability gap. The Treasury Board Automated Decision-Making Directive requires impact checklists inside federal ministries. Yet stalled federal bills left private AI under a voluntary code with zero stop-order powers.
