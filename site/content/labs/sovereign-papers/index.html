Five-section sovereign governance papers show how thirty authorities pair statutory airlocks, citizen vaults, signed agent roads, zero-knowledge treaties, and public compute to put citizens first. When cities first wired high-voltage electricity into homes and factories, private power trusts wrote their own safety codes while municipal grids lagged behind.

Fires and blackouts stopped only when public utility commissions enforced independent breaker standards, insulated home fuse boxes, and universal household service ahead of private monopoly profit. Governing frontier AI requires that same public-utility backbone. Today, leading states risk failing on two opposite edges. Some defer to corporate self-grading until an emergency triggers panic internet shutdowns, while others centralize state surveillance and race rivals in the dark.1,2,4

For researchers: administrative law, compute verification, and polycentric treaty design

Across rival geopolitical blocs and domestic administrative states, AI regulation fails when statutes rely either on unverified vendor prose or on centralized panoptic data collection. Under the Scrutable State doctrine (Caputo, 20262) and EU Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment templates (Mantelero, 20243), state agencies forfeit lawful deference when delegating public decisions to black-box contractors. CIN anchors sovereign governance across five sociotechnical primitives. First, a multimodal perceptual sensorium fuses observations in hardware Device Time with self-assessed observation confidence and silicon attestation (PUF, TCG DICE, DMTF SPDM, IETF RATS RFC 9334), optical data diodes, and IEC 61508 dead-man relays. Second, a local append-only C2PA/Merkle episodic memory vault (Endel Tulving; Tulving, 1972) enforces transduction realism (Signed Is Not True, Fu & Xu, 2018) across the analog hole (/labs/sensor-boundary/). Third, a monogamous 1:1 principal-bound personal cognitive model and cybernetic exocortex (Joscha Bach; Bach, 2009; Andy Clark & David Chalmers; Clark & Chalmers, 1998) acts as a statutory information fiduciary (Jack Balkin; Balkin, 2016) protecting unassisted competence. Fourth, bifurcated provenance ledgers across the personal Markov blanket (Karl Friston; Friston, 2013) separate the Private Autobiographical Ledger from the Public Attestation & Escrow Ledger (Public Attestation and Escrow Ledger) using selective ZK proofs, IETF SCITT RFC 9943 receipts, expiring Object-Capability capability lease sandboxes (Dennis & Van Horn, 1966), and independent-class k >= 3f + 1 Byzantine corroboration (/labs/protocol-inspector/, /labs/shared-horizon/). Local software synthesis (Malo et al., 20266) pairs with reciprocal mutual credit clearing (James Stodder; Stodder, 2009), statutory Rudolf Meidner (Meidner) cooperative equity, and bottom-up Elinor Ostrom (Ostrom) self-rule (Korinek & Stiglitz, 20177; Ostrom, 20108).
