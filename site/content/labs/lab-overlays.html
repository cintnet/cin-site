Financial independence does not mean isolation; we engage 43 main AI labs and 50 safety groups openly across six future scenarios while keeping zero commercial vetoes and letting living people write their own rules. Before ships from forty-three rival freight lines share one crowded harbour channel, the port master lays a clear tracing sheet over every fleet's chart. The sheet shows where each hull clears the stone lock, where its rudder swings wide, and which local pilot boards to keep small fishing boats safe.

We apply that same transparent overlay across forty-three main AI and general-intelligence labs and fifty safety institutes and alignment research groups. Financial independence never means sitting in a corner by ourselves. We take zero corporate equity and allow zero commercial vetoes. At the same time, we engage model builders and safety evaluators in daylight to show where our safeguards converge and where they diverge.

We also refuse to act as top-down lawmakers over society. Our single foundational goal is an exploratory agent loyal to its one human user. As each agent reports honest signal to a shared ledger, living people and their agents shape policies from the ground up.4 Below, a specialized liaison-agent template maps our sixty-nine working papers into six plain scenarios for each organisation, paired with dedicated per-lab technical papers for twelve flagship labs.

For researchers: formal mapping between the six scenario overlays and CIN's 69 working papers

Each organization record in research/institutions/lab_overlays_90.json instantiates a specialized liaison-agent specification across six structural scenarios distilled from Tracks 1 through 7 of the 69-paper research corpus:

  • Scenario 01 (Second Internet & SOTA Physical Airlock): Isolates frontier model weights inside audited compute vaults communicating exclusively over signed agent channels with control-data taint separation (Debenedetti et al., 20252), attested short-lived capability leases (Ding et al., 20265), and out-of-band hardware dead-man relays.
  • Scenario 02 (Shared Transparent Ledger Across Six Societal Domains): Couples append-only multi-witness execution logs with zero-knowledge principal and task-sponsor anonymity across legal, corporate, humanitarian, political, economic, and diplomatic institutions, aligned with polycentric monitoring principles (Ostrom, 19901).
  • Scenario 03 (Single-User Exploratory Agent & Emergent Self-Rule): Enforces a strict 1 living human : 1 principal-bound exploratory agent harness so collective policies emerge from bottom-up preference and lived-satisfaction signals over top-down foundation decrees (Huang et al., 20244).
  • Scenarios 04–06 (Cooperative Merit Tasking, Shadow-Ledger Containment & Modality-Bounded Deliberation): Evaluates identity-blinded task commissioning paired with gradual cooperative share dilution, adversarial control and substation power-balance audits against covert shadow ledgers (Greenblatt et al., 20233), and Sybil-resistant deliberation weighted by unique human-anchored lived context.
  • Five-primitive sociotechnical deliverable alignment: Scenario overlays trace five primitives across all 93 groups. Bifurcated ledgers across the personal Markov blanket (Karl Friston; Friston, 2013) separate the Private Autobiographical Ledger from the Public Attestation & Escrow Ledger (Public Attestation and Escrow Ledger) using selective ZK proofs, IETF SCITT (RFC 9943) receipts, expiring Object-Capability and capability lease gates (Jack B.
