Why rising automation stalls household demand under concentrated ownership, how cooperative share dilution and mutual credit keep capacity working, and how open receipts collapse secretive cartels are the core questions this workbench tests. Imagine a town bakery where automated ovens gradually bake ninety-five percent of the bread. If three absentee owners hold every share of the bakery, local families lose paycheques and cannot buy the loaves, leaving warm ovens idle beside empty tables.

We built two reproducible economic models in our multi-agent sandbox to test practical fixes before recommending any rule. You can adjust every parameter below, test extreme edge cases, and inspect the primary economic studies behind each curve.

What these two models test

1. Keeping households in the loop as factories automate

In a concentrated economy, automating ninety-five percent of routine work severs the link between factory output and family income. Machines can assemble goods all day, yet households lack the paycheques to clear the shelves.

Combining gradual cooperative share issuance1 with an essentials floor and a zero-sum mutual credit clearinghouse2,3 keeps purchasing power moving without speculative coins. Positive clearing balances gradually expire so nobody can hoard standing claims, and every credit issued to a producer matches a debit elsewhere on the ledger.

To prevent automated bot farms from gaming product scores, 30-day durability signals collapse onto verified human accounts. Spawning five hundred extra software agents under one person adds zero extra weight.

2. Making secretive cartels unstable from the inside

Open cooperatives grow stronger as more members pool demand forecasts and safety checks, an effect formalised in Roger Myerson's cooperative graph theory.5 Locking shared demand graphs behind verifiable receipts denies free-riders a free lunch without turning the network into a personal surveillance system.

When border opacity margins and physical power-draw checks pair with Joseph Harrington's antitrust leniency rule,4 a hidden cartel loses internal trust. The first insider to hand over cryptographic proof of price-fixing receives amnesty and a safe-harbor credit paid from co-conspirator fines, turning collusion into a race to defect.

Honest limits

Stylised macroeconomic and game-theory equations help us spot broken incentives early, before writing software or proposing law. They simplify real supply chains, regional politics, and human habits, so every rule tested here still needs small, democratic real-world trials. For researchers: conservation laws, submodular weighting, and Nash thresholds

Both interactive panels mirror the deterministic Python simulation suite in research/ledger_sim/cooperative_dark_ledger_sim.py, verified with common-random-number seeds so regime comparisons never suffer from random-number phase drift:

  • Meidner equity dilution and blinded commissioning: Above automation threshold a_0 = 0.30, private equity dilutes via share issuance P_{t+1} = P_t / (1 + delta * max(0, a_t - a_0) + c_blind * a_t), with P_t + C_t = 1 identically at every step (Meidner, 1978).1
  • Zero-sum capacity-backed mutual credit with demurrage: Multilateral clearing balances are tracked in integer micro-credits across households and producers. Periodic demurrage decay on positive balances amortizes negative mutual obligations symmetrically so sum(balances) == 0 holds within 1e-9 at every step (Stodder, 2009; Iosifidis et al., 2018).2,3
  • Sybil-resistant 1-human-root submodular modality weighting: Multi-agent 30-day durability attestations collapse onto verified human roots before applying f(M) = (sum_{m in M} w_m)^gamma with 0 < gamma < 1, satisfying strict diminishing marginal returns and exact zero Sybil gain.
  • Closed-form Harrington cartel collapse threshold: Physical boundary checks compare metered draw against receipted compute (E_in = E_accounted + E_shadow + epsilon) to set catch rate p_phys. Unilateral whistleblower defection strictly dominates staying silent whenever safe-harbor credit B exceeds max(0, (pi_C(tau) - pi_O - p_phys * F) / (1 - beta * (1 - p_phys))) (Harrington, 2008; Myerson, 1977).4,5
  • Five-primitive sociotechnical architecture coupling: Macroeconomic and dark-ledger models operate across five primitives. A multimodal egocentric and ambient sensorium fuses signals in hardware Device Time with self-assessed observation confidence, silicon attestation (PUF, TCG DICE, DMTF SPDM, IETF RATS RFC 9334), an optical data diode (optical data diodes), and IEC 61508 dead-man relays. A local append-only C2PA and Merkle episodic memory vault (Endel Tulving, 1972) enforces transduction realism because Signed Is Not True across the analog hole (Kevin Fu and Wenyuan Xu, 2018; /labs/sensor-boundary/). A monogamous 1:1 personal cognitive model and cybernetic exocortex (Joscha Bach, 2009) draws on the Extended Mind thesis (Andy Clark and David Chalmers; Clark & Chalmers, 1998) and the Good Regulator theorem (Conant & Ashby, 1970). It applies interventional causal models do(x) (Pearl, 2009) and compiler-trust reflection proofs (Thompson, 1984) as a statutory information fiduciary (Jack Balkin; Balkin, 2016) that preserves unassisted competence. Bifurcated ledgers across the personal Markov blanket (Karl Friston; Friston, 2010; Friston, 2013) separate the Private Autobiographical Ledger from the Public Attestation & Escrow Ledger (Public Attestation and Escrow Ledger).
