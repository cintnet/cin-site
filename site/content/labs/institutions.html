How to read the five governance dimensions

Every dossier in this directory rates public governance practices across five practical questions grounded in verifiable operating rules. Higher scores reflect checkable institutional duties that outside citizens, auditors, or members can verify today.

  • Oversight (1 to 5): Whether independent boards, public regulators, or workers hold real power to pause or veto unsafe deployments.1,2
  • Action receipts (1 to 5): Whether models, datasets, and automated tools leave signed, tamper-evident records for incident review.2
  • Shared capacity (1 to 5): Whether compute, open weights, standards, and economic gains spread across communities through open access and shared stewardship.1,4
  • Independent safety testing (1 to 5): Whether external red teams and public safety institutes can test systems before release without executive veto.2,3
  • Public voice (1 to 5): Whether affected residents, union stewards, and cooperative members help set operational rules before tools go live.1,4
