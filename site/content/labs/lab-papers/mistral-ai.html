Section 01 · Architecture and Alignment Bottlenecks

Open-weight velocity, uncontestable ministry connectors, and compute asymmetry

Mistral AI balances Apache-licensed sparse Mixture-of-Experts releases with closed commercial Mistral Large and sovereign defense deployments. Operating with smaller private GPU clusters than US and Chinese hyperscalers also creates pressure to rent foreign clouds or compress safety checks during rapid capability jumps.

Split open and closed systemic-risk incentives

Balancing rapid open-weight releases with closed enterprise tiers leaves gaps in published autonomous-subversion testing and training-lineage transparency.3

Connecting Le Chat and Mistral Large to administrative files without signed audit receipts leaves citizens unable to challenge automated decisions.

Smaller private accelerator budgets create pressure either to depend on foreign hyperscaler clouds or rush unverified coding checkpoints into critical industry.
