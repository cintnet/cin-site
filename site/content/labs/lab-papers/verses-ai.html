Section 02 · CIN Technical Integration

Asymmetric human loyalty and split boundary ledgers

CIN resolves the free-energy symmetry by binding each personal Genius agent one-to-one with a single living person under asymmetric loyalty rules. The agent must update its own internal beliefs to track its person's wishes, while hardware rules forbid nudging, deskilling, or narrowing human choices.

CIN also implements Friston's statistical boundary1 as two separated provenance ledgers. Raw health and private Bayesian beliefs stay encrypted at home, while external Spatial Web calls use minimal proofs and expiring capability leases4.

At the community scale, polycentric self-rule5 keeps collective coordination voluntary. Neighborhoods gain wider foresight2 while preserving each person's right to inspect, contest, or exit shared domain rules.
