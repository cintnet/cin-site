When early steamships crossed the Atlantic, each shipping line trusted its own boiler gauges and kept its hull blueprints locked in a company safe. Boilers kept bursting until independent marine engineers published open pressure-valve standards, load-line marks, and mutual inspection rules tailored to each vessel class.

Today's frontier AI builders face a harder version of that pressure problem. Dedicated five-section technical papers below show how forty-three language-model and general-intelligence labs can pair local 1:1 user vaults, signed capability leases, mutual verification receipts, and cooperative compute shares to adopt advanced AI safely. For researchers: cross-paradigm synthesis of the five-section protocol stack

Across autoregressive language models, flow-matching robotic policies, 3D scene generators, Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures, and variational free-energy agents, internal alignment objectives fail whenever an optimizer can modify its own reward channel, exploit ambient OAuth credentials, or coerce its environment to reduce prediction error (Greenblatt et al., 20231; Bengio et al., 20255; Friston, 20136). CIN resolves this structurally across five sociotechnical primitives. First, a multimodal perceptual sensorium fuses observations in hardware Device Time with silicon attestation (PUF, TCG DICE, DMTF SPDM, IETF RATS RFC 9334), optical data diodes, and IEC 61508 dead-man relays. Second, a local append-only C2PA/Merkle episodic memory vault enforces transduction realism across the analog hole (/labs/sensor-boundary/). Third, a monogamous 1:1 principal-bound personal cognitive model acts as a statutory information fiduciary. Fourth, bifurcated provenance ledgers across the personal Markov blanket separate the Private Autobiographical Ledger from the Public Attestation & Escrow Ledger using IETF SCITT receipts and hardware-attested expiring capability leases (Ding et al., 20262; /labs/protocol-inspector/). Fifth, polycentric coordination on the Wide Network Ledger couples Universal Basic Services, hardware-rooted compute proofs (Sastry et al., 20243), local program synthesis (Malo et al., 20264), statutory Meidner cooperative equity, reciprocal mutual-credit clearing (Korinek & Stiglitz, 20177; Iosifidis et al., 20188), and bottom-up Ostrom self-rule.
