Section 01 · Architecture & Alignment Bottlenecks

Why 3D world models create distinct privacy and physical safety risks

Language models process flat strings of text and routinely lose track of where solid objects sit when a camera turns away. World Labs solves this geometric blindness with Large World Models that maintain persistent three-dimensional scenes, material surfaces, and physical mechanics across time.

Moving from text chat to continuous spatial modeling introduces three severe safety bottlenecks. Cloud depth scans map private rooms, sim-to-real robotics loops trigger sudden manipulation jumps, and optical spoofing corrupts plans before silicon signatures apply.

Total spatial privacy enclosure

Smart glasses and home robots scanning hallways in 3D capture medicine cabinets, door locks, and family routines. Streaming raw meshes to multi-tenant clouds builds a permanent map of private life.

Sim-to-real active-learning overhangs

When agents pair differentiable 3D physics rollouts with targeted physical robot probes, dexterity and mechanical bypass skills jump abruptly. Static pre-release benchmarks miss this rapid transfer loop.

Sensor spoofing across the analog hole

Timed laser pulses or ultrasonic interference can inject phantom walls or erase real obstacles before analog-to-digital conversion. Planners that trust signed feeds blindly risk dangerous collisions.

Unlabelled synthetic scene feedback

Photorealistic 3D renders re-entering training sets or civil inspections can masquerade as real physical evidence. Downstream engineering tools then plan against fabricated structural geometry.

For researchers: SE(3) spatial state spaces and active-learning sample complexity

World Labs Large World Models represent environments as persistent metric scene graphs and 3D Gaussian or signed-distance primitives evolving under differentiable transition dynamics s_{t+1} = f_theta(s_t, u_t). While virtual code benchmarks scale visibly first, closed-loop active learning over f_theta dramatically compresses real-world robotic sample complexity once manipulators probe uncertain contact friction or kinematic joints directly. Across the pre-conversion analog hole, cryptographic peripheral signatures verify silicon firmware custody after signal digitization, never unspoofed physical ground truth.

Section 02 · CIN Technical Integration

Sealing raw 3D scans at home while proving safe clearance

CIN anchors spatial intelligence inside local-first hardware custody1. Wearable cameras, radar, and motion sensors align to a local capture clock and send raw 3D scans across optical diodes into an encrypted vault.

Only the resident and their bound single-person assistant can decrypt that spatial history. When an outside robot or World Labs planner needs navigation bounds, the home assistant exports a zero-knowledge proof confirming clearance without revealing furniture or people2.

Before any simulated plan drives physical motors, CIN enforces strict control-and-data separation so adversarial visual scenes cannot hijack tool permissions3. Every motor handoff requires an expiring hardware capability lease paired with a physical dead-man switch4.

Cross-modal sensor cross-checking

Local edge fusion compares optical depth against millimeter-wave radar and inertial motion on Device Time. Disagreement widens uncertainty bars and halts motion before spoofed meshes corrupt planning.

Zero-knowledge geometric bounds

Cryptographic proofs verify that a doorway meets wheelchair width or a floor supports load limits. External World Labs models never see private room layouts2.

Isolated perception and control channels

Untrusted 3D geometry updates obstacle maps without ever issuing privileged system commands. Visual or spatial prompt injections fail deterministically at the type boundary3.

Expiring hardware actuator leases

Simulated trajectories compile into sub-second capability leases capping force, speed, and spatial reach, backed by hardware dead-man relays that cut power if renewal stops4.

For researchers: Markov blanket ZK predicates, Object-Capability leases, and Sensorium attestation

At the Perceptual Sensorium root, PUF, TCG DICE, DMTF SPDM, and IETF RATS bind RGB-D and radar streams to attested silicon before writing C2PA Merkle records into the local vault1. Across the Personal Markov Blanket, the principal-bound Exocortex proves collision-free polytope predicates via succinct zero-knowledge arguments2. Physical actuation combines CaMeL dataflow isolation3 with ACLE-MCP Object-Capability leases4 and IEC 61508 hardware safety relays.

Section 03 · Inter-Lab Coordination Options

Shared sim-to-real evaluations and synthetic 3D provenance

No single lab can gauge physical robotics risk behind redacted internal reports. World Labs can partner with Physical Intelligence, AMI Labs, Google DeepMind, and public safety institutes on open sim-to-real active-learning evaluations.

Participating labs also benefit from shared cryptographic receipts that separate synthetic 3D world renders from attested camera captures. Hardware-enforced cluster quorums protect high-cost spatial model weights against rapid credential theft and exfiltration5.

Joint sim-to-real overhang benchmarks

Measure how few physical robot trials a 3D world model needs to master unfamiliar latches, tools, or deformable materials, triggering shared containment tiers early.

SCITT synthetic-versus-captured receipts

Stamp every generated 3D scene on an append-only transparency ledger so downstream safety planners and future training runs never confuse synthetic renders with camera capture.

For researchers: closed-loop transfer metrics and cluster weight-exfiltration quorums

Standard visual fidelity scores such as PSNR miss closed-loop manipulation overhangs; joint benchmarks track active system-identification sample complexity across independent robotic testbeds. To prevent 12-to-48-hour monorepo weight leaks, shard export requires DMTF SPDM enclave attestation and multi-party cryptographic quorum sign-off5 alongside IETF SCITT provenance receipts.

Section 04 · Government & Sovereign Engagement Options

Inspectable municipal digital twins without street-to-bedroom surveillance

Cities, transit agencies, and flood responders need accurate 3D world models to plan evacuations, seismic upgrades, and wheelchair-accessible routes. Handing street-to-bedroom LiDAR maps to private cloud vendors creates unacceptable surveillance risks.

Running World Labs models on public civic compute lets municipalities maintain open, auditable digital twins of public streets that meet administrative law standards6. Private homes and clinics connect only through zero-knowledge fire-safety or utility clearance proofs, while compute governance tracks frontier training runs5.

Scrutable civic digital twins

Host municipal 3D models of bridges, transit hubs, and floodplains on public compute so engineers and residents can audit simulation assumptions directly6.

Critical infrastructure twin airlocks

Require hardware-attested operator identity, expiring capability leases, and public audit logs before any external agent queries high-detail electrical substation or water-plant twins.

For researchers: administrative scrutability and sovereign compute verification

Deploying municipal Large World Models on sovereign civic clusters satisfies administrative due-process requirements for a scrutable state by logging physical solver tolerances and boundary conditions6. High-FLOP spatial pre-training clusters publish privacy-preserving workload attestations5 while enforcing asymmetric transparency on critical civil infrastructure queries.
