Collective Intelligence Network

One personal AI on your side, and an open ledger for working together.

CIN designs open infrastructure uniting wearable and home sensors in a local memory vault, a 1:1 personal assistant loyal to you, and dual signed ledgers for collective tasks. Physical sensors can still be fooled, so a single sensor reading does not count as automatic proof. A 1:1 personal model bound to your wishes and wellbeing keeps a private ledger at home. For shared work, a public ledger on a separate network for agents shares only minimal proofs, temporary permissions, and verifiable checks.

Home custody

Local memory vault and honest sensors

Trusted wearable and room sensors log daily context into an encrypted vault on your own hardware. Because noisy or spoofed sensors happen in the physical world, hardware signatures cannot verify physical truth alone.

For researchers: five-primitive sociotechnical architecture and primary sources

Our open blueprints unite five concrete sociotechnical primitives across hardware attestation, personal cognitive modeling, and polycentric coordination. First, a multimodal egocentric and ambient perceptual sensorium fuses wearable and room sensors locally in monotonic hardware Device Time with self-assessed observation confidence. It anchors readings in silicon peripheral attestation (PUF, TCG DICE, DMTF SPDM, IETF RATS RFC 93341), an optical data diode (optical data diodes), and IEC 61508 dead-man relays. Second, a cryptographically sealed local append-only C2PA1 and Merkle episodic memory vault (Endel Tulving; Tulving, 19722) encrypts history strictly for the biological person and their bound model. It enforces transduction realism (Signed Is Not True across the analog hole; Kevin Fu and Wenyuan Xu; Fu & Xu, 2018, doi:10.1145/31764021).

Third, a monogamous 1:1 principal-bound personal cognitive model and cybernetic exocortex (Joscha Bach; Bach, 20092) draws on the Extended Mind thesis (Andy Clark and David Chalmers; Clark & Chalmers, 19982). It applies the Good Regulator theorem (Conant & Ashby, 19703), interventional causal models do(x) (Pearl, 20093), and compiler-trust reflection proofs (Thompson, 19843) as a statutory information fiduciary (Jack Balkin; Balkin, 20164) protecting unassisted competence. Fourth, bifurcated provenance ledgers across the personal Markov blanket (Karl Friston; Friston, 2010; Friston, 20134) partition the Private Autobiographical Ledger from the Public Attestation & Escrow Ledger (Public Attestation and Escrow Ledger). External transitions use selective zero-knowledge (ZK) proofs, IETF SCITT (RFC 99435) receipts, expiring Object-Capability and capability lease bounds (Jack B. Dennis and Earl C. Van Horn; Dennis & Van Horn, 19665), and independent-class Byzantine corroboration (k >= 3f + 15). Fifth, a polycentric collective coordination substrate couples sovereign dyads to the Wide Network Ledger for Universal Basic Services, merit-bonded commons tasks, and plural value accounting (Jacob Meluso; Meluso et al., 20226). It combines countercyclical reciprocal mutual credit (James Stodder; Stodder, 20096), statutory cooperative equity (Rudolf Meidner; Meidner, 19787), frontier-lab overlays and public governance graphs (Bracale Syrnikov et al., 20267), and bottom-up self-rule (Elinor Ostrom; Ostrom, 19908). Inspect interactive models in Protocol Inspector, Sensor Boundary, and Shared Horizon.
