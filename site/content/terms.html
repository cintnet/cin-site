Using this site

Ideas only help if people can read, test, and argue with them. You're welcome to read, share, and link to anything on this site. All we ask is that you don't misrepresent what we've written or suggest we endorse something we don't.

Our writing

Unless a page says otherwise, you can quote and share our writing freely. Just give credit and link back to the original page. When we publish formal specifications across our vision and interactive labs, we include an open licence so you know how you can build on them.

No advice, no warranties

Everything here is research and opinion, not legal, financial, medical, or professional advice. We work hard to get the facts right, but we can't promise every page is complete or current. When we get something wrong, we fix it openly on our corrections page.

Links

We link to other people's work so you can read our sources firsthand and give credit where it's due. A link is never a partnership or an endorsement, and we aren't responsible for outside websites.

Contact

If you're unsure how you can use our work, visit Support, explore ways to get involved, or ask us at contact@collectiveintelligencenetwork.org. A person reads every message, and we're happy to help you share or build on these ideas. Last updated: November 6, 2026.
