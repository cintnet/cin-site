Want to talk with us about anything published on cintnet.org? The best way to reach us is by email at contact@collectiveintelligencenetwork.org.

A person reads every message, rather than routing you through an automated inbox. Write to us if you think we are wrong on our public corrections ledger, have a question not covered on Support, or know of existing work we missed.

You can also inspect our open code, datasets, and benchmarks on GitHub, or join live community discussions in our Discord server. Subscribe via our Atom/RSS feed for new essays.

We are based in Ottawa, Canada. Read our vision, learn more about our workshop, test prototypes in our labs, or explore ways to get involved across public policy, alignment research, AI labs, and community organising.
